About — In His Own Words

For the better part of a decade I have worked where engineering meets design: the narrow seam where a clear idea either survives its collision with real constraints or quietly comes apart. The discipline I trust most is subtraction — building the smallest honest version of a thing, then sharpening it until nothing extra remains.

That instinct has carried across developer tooling, fintech infrastructure, and consumer products. The surfaces look different; the method does not. Fewer moving parts. Sharper defaults. Interfaces that explain themselves without a manual.

I care about the unglamorous middle of a project — the part after the demo and before the launch, where most of the real decisions get made. That is where good products are won or lost, and it is the part I enjoy the most.

“The best interface is the one you never have to think about.”
